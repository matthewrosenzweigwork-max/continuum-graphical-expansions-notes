# Graphical Expansions in Continuum Statistical Mechanics
## Combinatorics, Analysis, and Alternative Representations

Graduate lecture notes for PDE/probability students and researchers, with no substantial prior graph-combinatorics or statistical-mechanics background assumed.

**Status:** repository and production scaffold established. Literature audit, theorem freeze, mathematical drafting, and independent review are pending. No completed lectures or verified mathematical results are claimed.

- Six 90-minute core lectures, with a target of roughly 75–100 pages; complete proofs take priority over the page target.
- Two advanced workshops connecting classical expansions with forest interpolation and singular-interaction asymptotics.
- Emphasis on continuum models and on genuinely equivalent representations, not merely analogous diagrams.
